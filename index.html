Logic Gates

Two-gene combination scoring

SCART evaluates three logic-gate strategies for two-gene CAR-T combinations, each with different safety–efficacy tradeoffs.

A & B

AND gate

Both genes must be co-expressed for CAR-T activation. Dramatically reduces on-target off-tumour toxicity. Best safety profile.

Safety
High
A | B

OR gate

Either gene expression triggers activation. Maximises tumour coverage in heterogeneous tumours. Higher efficacy, broader target.

Efficacy
High
A &! B

NOT gate

Gene A expressed AND gene B absent. Targets tumour-specific loss-of-expression patterns. Useful for tumour suppressor contexts.

Specificity
High
